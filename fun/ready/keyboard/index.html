Ready

Keyboard

Ready's keys, set out as a keyboard. They lift as you pass over them and push in when you click, tap or type.

Sculpted keys

Each key stands on sloped sides with a dished top set in from the edge. It rises a pixel under the pointer and drops 6 px into its hole when pressed.

Upper case legends

Legends are upper case and white, in Ready Book; the word keys are big and wide-set. On number and symbol keys the shifted character sits above and takes over while shift is held.

One key in colour

Dark keys in a beige case, and only return in orange. Caps lock and shift light a small orange lamp.
